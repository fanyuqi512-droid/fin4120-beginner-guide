§ 1. MOTIVATION FOR INVESTING

讲义原文

1. MOTIVATION FOR INVESTING

1. MOTIVATION FOR INVESTING

1. Motivation for International Bonds

• Diversification: Don’t Put All Eggs in One
  Basket
   – spreading investment across different
     assets to lower risk
   – Bonus: Can unlock new sources of return
     (alpha) beyond your home market.

• Currency hedging:
   – Hedged portfolios remove currency
     swings and can focus on local interest
     rate & yields.
   – Unhedged portfolios keep currency
     exposure (potential win or lose based on
     FX changes).                                       Diversification ≠ Hedging
解析

投国际债的动机：分散单一国家利率/信用风险、寻找更高实际收益、匹配外币负债等。

但分散化会被汇率波动「搅局」——后面必须谈 hedging。

§ Summary & Key Takeaways

讲义原文

Summary & Key Takeaways

Summary & Key Takeaways

• Diversification & Yield: International bonds offer risk reduction and alpha
  via currency/rate differentials (e.g., U.S. firms borrowing in euros).
• Market Types:
   – Internal: Domestic (local issuers) and foreign (e.g., Yankee, Panda
       bonds).
   – External: Eurobonds (issued offshore) and global bonds (multi-market).
• FX Risk: Returns hinge on currency movements—hedge via forwards,
  swaps, or natural/proxy hedges.
• Structures: Cross-border bonds feature multi-currency clauses, UK law, and
  Euroclear/Clearstream settlement.
• Indices: Track sovereign (FTSE WGBI), aggregate (Bloomberg Global Agg),
  and EM (JPM EMBI)
