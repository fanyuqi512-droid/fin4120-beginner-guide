•   (inverse) If yield goes up, price goes down,

    the price gain from a decrease in yield is
    larger than the price loss from an

•   (convex) At high yields → bond price
    becomes less sensitive to further increases

3. Bond Price Change Over Time

3. Bond Price Change Over Time
What causes bond prices to change?
Three main reasons:
1. Credit Risk Change
    – If issuer gets riskier, investors demand higher yield → price drops.
    – If safer, yield falls → price rises.
2. Pull-to-Par (Time Effect)
    – Discount bond? Price slowly rises to 1,000.
    – Premium bond? Price slowly falls to1,000.
           All bonds "pull" toward par as maturity nears.
3. Market Yield Changes
    – Rates go up? Old bond prices drop.
    – Rates go down? Old bond prices rise.
          (They adjust to stay competitive.)
解析

即使收益率不变，折价债会随时间向面值靠拢（pull to par）；溢价债则逐渐回落至面值。

• When someone sells a bond, they don’t wait for the next coupon

§ Summary & Key Takeaways

讲义原文

Summary & Key Takeaways

Summary & Key Takeaways

• Bond pricing: A vanilla bond is worth the present value of future
  coupons and principal, discounted at the yield.
• Price-yield relation: Bond prices move inversely to yields, with a
  convex curve that favors price gains when yields fall.
• Price over time: Even if yields stay the same, bond prices pull toward
  par as maturity approaches.
• Floaters vs. Inverse floaters: Floating-rate bonds adjust coupons with
  the market, while inverse floaters gain when rates drop.
• Quoted prices: Investors pay the clean price plus accrued interest—
  the total is the dirty price.
