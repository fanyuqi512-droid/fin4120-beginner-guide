§ 1. WHAT IS A BOND

讲义原文

1. WHAT IS A BOND

1. What Is A Bond
What is a bond?
A bond = an "I.O.U." from a borrower to you (the investor)
• The borrower promises:
    – Regular Interest Payments → coupon payments
        • Usually paid every 6 months.
    – Return of Your Original Money → principal or “face value”
        • Paid back on the maturity date (the end), usually $1000 per bond.

The payment structure
  Time (t)        1             2                     3    ...       N
Cash Flow         𝐶1           𝐶2                     𝐶3   …       𝐶𝑁 + 𝐹
    – 𝑪𝒕 = coupon payment each period
    – 𝑭 = face value (principal), repaid at maturity (time N)
解析

债券 = 借款人给你的 I.O.U.（欠条）：承诺定期付票息（coupon），到期还本金（principal / face / par）。

现金流时间线：前 N−1 期收票息 C；最后一期收 C + 面值 F。美国公司债常见每半年付一次息。

CF_t = C (t < N); CF_N = C + F
易错点：「固定收益」≠ 零风险：票息合同可固定，但市价、通胀、违约、汇率仍会变。

解析

四大零件：Par Value（面值）、Coupon Rate（年票息率）、Maturity（到期）、Payment Frequency（付息频率）。

每期票息 ≈（票息率 × 面值）/ 年付息次数。美国常见面值 $1,000；中国国债常见面值 ¥100。

易错点：票息率是合同写死的；收益率（yield）是按现价算出来的——两者只有在平价时才常相等。

看监管是为了理解：谁批准发行、谁管交易场所、违约时谁介入——这会影响流动性与信息透明度。

5. BOND IDENTIFICATION AND

5. BOND IDENTIFICATION AND

5. Bond Identification & Quoting

Naming Convention

E.g.: in swift
                   Coca-Cola 4.25s 5/15/2029
Means: 4.25% coupon, matures May 15, 2029

Translation: A Coca-Cola bond that pays 4.25% interest and matures on May
15, 2029.

Note: the notation does not tell the payment frequency, yet most of the US
corporate bonds are paid semiannually
解析

报价记号例如「Coca-Cola 4.25s 5/15/2029」= 发行人 + 票息率 + 到期日。美国常用 CUSIP 识别码；跨境常用更长的 CINS 等。

记号往往不写付息频率——但美债公司债默认多半是半年付息，做题时要确认。

§ Summary & Key Takeaways

讲义原文

Summary & Key Takeaways

Summary & Key Takeaways

• Bond = Loan contract with fixed payments + return of
  principal.
• Key parts: Par Value, Coupon Rate, Maturity, Payment
  Frequency.
• Market split into sectors: Treasury, Corporate, Municipal,
  ABS/MBS, etc.
• Bonds can include embedded options (call, put, convert) and
  variable coupon structures.
• Core risks: Interest rate, credit, reinvestment, inflation,
  liquidity, and etc.
• Bottom line: Understand structure and risks to invest wisely
解析
