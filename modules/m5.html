§ 1. THREE PILLARS OF

讲义原文

1. THREE PILLARS OF

1. Three Pillars of Corporate Debt
Corporate debt can be broadly categorized into three key funding sources:
(1) Bank Loans (private credit),
(2) Money Market Debt (short-term instruments), and
(3) Capital Market Debt (long-term securities)

These instruments are further categorized by sector:
• Utilities (electric, gas, water)
• Financials (banks, insurers, mortgage firms, etc.)
• Industrials (all other corporations)

In this slide, we will focus on corporate bonds, MTNs, Commercial Papers,
and Bank Loans. Convertible bonds and asset-backed securities are
addressed in other chapters.
解析

公司债务三大支柱常被概括为：公司债券、中期票据（MTN）、商业票据（CP）/银行贷款等融资渠道——讲义按工具类型展开。

不同支柱在期限、信息披露、投资者群体、监管上不同，企业会「期限匹配」地选择融资。

§ 3. CORPORATE DEFAULT RISK

讲义原文

3. CORPORATE DEFAULT RISK

3. CORPORATE DEFAULT RISK

3. Corporate Debt Ratings
(a) Credit risk vs. default risk
  Default Risk = The Risk of Missing A Payment or Can’t Pay At All.
  • Example: A company goes bankrupt and stops paying interest on its
    bonds.

  Credit Risk = The Overall Risk of Losing Money
  • It includes multiple types of risk, not just missing payments.
  • Can lead to financial loss even if no default happens (for example?).

Question: think of any risk that is credit risk but not default risk?
解析

评级是违约风险的「速记符号」；notch（如 A+ / A / A−）是微调。投资级 vs 投机级（高收益）是重要分界。

评级是滞后且离散的；市场信用利差往往更早反映恶化。历史违约率与回收率（recovery）用来粗估期望损失。

Approx. expected loss ≈ default rate × (1 − recovery)

§ 6. COMMERCIAL PAPER

讲义原文

6. COMMERCIAL PAPER

6. Commercial Paper
A commercial paper (CP) 短期融资券is a short-term, unsecured
promissory note issued by corporations to finance working capital or
short-term liabilities.
• Commercial Paper (CP) is NOT considered a bond, but rather a
   short-term money market instrument.
解析

破产清偿大致优先级：Secured（有担保）≻ Unsecured senior ≻ Subordinated ≻ Equity。

同一层内部还可能有契约细节（担保品、保证、结构性后偿）。评级与价差高度依赖你在瀑布中的位置。

易错点：「高级债」若无担保，仍可能损失惨重；看的是有效优先级与回收率，不只是名字。
